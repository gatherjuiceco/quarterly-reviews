Patterns

  • The execution stayed strong while direction-setting thinned. Task output held steady, but September lost its monthly goals and half its needle-movers, and that was the month the numbers turned against you.

  • Every miss was something low-effort but uncomfortable. Your own outcome notes: the Berkley conversation was "opportunity but not the motivation", the BP readings "I honestly didn't even try", and the July budget sitting "didn't even really make a solid attempt."

  • Personal life got real, dated wins. Camping was booked and taken, there were outings with Gretchen, and opener training was designed. The One-Year Plan's personal weighting shows up in the results.

  • Your calendar shows you back on the floor. 7shifts has you on 10 shifts between 9/12 and 9/25, nine of them opens, in the same two weeks the 13 hours came off the schedule.
